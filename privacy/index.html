Privacy Policy

Retrospective doesn't collect any data about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app has no server of its own.

If you split an album By Place, the app asks Apple Maps for the name of each area your photos were taken in. Only a rounded location (an area about 28 km across) is sent, never a photo, and the names are kept on your device. Apple handles these requests under its own privacy policy.

The folders and albums you choose are read on your Apple Vision Pro to hang your photos. They never leave your device, and the app never changes or deletes them.

If your photos are in iCloud Drive or iCloud Photos, Apple downloads them to your device when the app needs them. That happens between your device and your own iCloud account; we can't see it.

Your settings, such as wall colours, favourites and which galleries are protected, are stored on your device.

Protected galleries open with Optic ID or your passcode. Apple handles that check; the app only learns whether it succeeded.

The purchase is handled by Apple. We don't receive your payment details.
