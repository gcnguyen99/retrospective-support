Getting your photos in

Tap Choose a Folder and pick a folder in Files or iCloud Drive. Photos directly in it form one gallery, and every folder inside it becomes a gallery of its own. You can also tap Photos and add albums from your Photos library.

New photos show up by themselves when you come back to the app. Photos that are still downloading from iCloud are counted in the window and hang once they're in.

In the exhibition

Tap Enter Exhibition, then pinch a doorway to walk into a gallery. Pinch a photo to hold it, swipe to see the next one, and pinch again to hang it back. Press the Digital Crown to leave.

The lectern holds the controls: the way back to the entrance, music, and the More menu with wall colour, frames and a new layout for the Pavilion.

Protected galleries

On a gallery card, tap … and protect the gallery. It then opens only after Optic ID or your passcode, and closes again when you leave the app.
